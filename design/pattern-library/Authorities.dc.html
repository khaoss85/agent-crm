The four authorities

This is the whole idea, and the one thing the Admin does not do today. Colour here is not a severity scale — warning, danger, success. It names which authority is holding the record right now. The site draws the same four as arcs closing into one seal.

Agent --agent #7457ff

Something a machine composed or proposed. Never something a machine decided.

proposal draft
Policy --pending #f2c84b

A deterministic rule decided, or is holding the gate. Nothing is wrong; something is bounded.

approval_required reject
Human --human #ff6b69

A named person must act, or has acted. The rarest colour on the screen, and the loudest.

rejected needs you
Evidence --data #20b8d8

Audit, trace, immutable identifiers. Things that can be replayed, not changed.

recorded immutable
Accord --accord #27b64a · words --accent #176b2a

Not a fifth authority — what the four produce when they agree. Two greens exist because one cannot do both jobs: #27b64a is a fill you read as a shape and measures 2.41:1 as 15px text on paper. Words are always #176b2a, which is 5.97:1.

The finding this taxonomy produces: three refusals, not one

The real discount policy returns three decisions, and two of them are refusals with different authors. Today the Admin paints both, plus a crashed run, in the same red.

reject The rule refused Above rejectAboveBps. Deterministic, and no human is being asked. Policy.
rejected A person refused Someone with a name looked at it and said no, with a recorded reason. Human.
failed Nothing refused The run broke. No decision was reached at all. A fault — the one state no authority owns.

The discipline

An authority colour may only appear where that authority is genuinely in play. Coral on a screen with no pending human decision is a lie the design tells before the copy gets a chance. If a state belongs to none of the four, it is ink, muted, or red — never a fifth hue.
